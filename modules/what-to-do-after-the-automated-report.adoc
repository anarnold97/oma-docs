= What to do after the automated report

[role="_abstract"]
An automated migration report is an input to a larger program, not the end of the work. After you are satisfied with the data that you submitted and the result in the {HCC}, you can do the following with your own organization and, if you have a contract, with {RH}:

* *Align owners* — Share the result with the platform, application, and security owners who will sign off on a target design.
* *Close gaps* — Address any high-severity items the report lists before you invest in a full {MTV} cutover plan. Some items will need a human decision even when the tool cannot automate a fix.
* *Design the target* — Build a final {OCP-short} and {OSV} design that includes networks, storage, identity, and Day 2 operations. The automated report supports that design; it does not replace it.
* *Engage {RH}* — If you have a {RH} account or support contract, use the support and services paths that are documented by your account team. Internal contact details or nonpublic programs are not included here.
